categories in sequence inside each. This departs from the handoff's "category IDs are not
renumbered" rule, on instruction, and is safe only because these ids were never public: the live
1.x site shipped `P1`–`P15` and never exposed a U-id.

Every category and pattern keeps `source_category_id`, and the full map is in

## Count semantics

- Pattern and factor counts are recomputed from unique edges, never copied from the catalogues'
  `n_papers`. Both are stored separately (`mapped_paper_count` vs `catalogue_n_papers`) and agree
  for all 83 patterns and for every sub-factor untouched by the revision below — independent
  confirmation that the edge files and the catalogues describe the same coding pass.
- Category counts are distinct papers. A paper holding two patterns from one category is counted once
  (explicitly tested).
- Factor counts collapse multiple sub-factor edges from the same paper into one.
- `pattern_x_factor` cells count distinct papers holding both, over all roles, per the current spec.
  Each cell is bounded by both margins, and the grand total exceeds 1,748 because both dimensions are
  many-to-many — that is expected, not an error.

## Deliberate departures from the handoff

Four of the handoff's stated constraints were superseded by data or instructions supplied
afterwards. Each was confirmed before being dropped.

0. **"Pattern IDs and category IDs are not renumbered."** Category ids were renumbered into reading
   order — see the section above. Pattern ids were left alone.

1. **"Preserve all 4,397 factor edges exactly."** `human_factors_papers.csv` is a fresh coding pass,
   not a remap: only 2,158 of the 1.x file's 4,394 factor edges survive into v2 (49%), and the
   primary/secondary distinction no longer exists. Confirmed as intended.
2. **"The 18-factor taxonomy is not being redesigned."** It grew to 23 with five new factors
   (N1–N5) and a 97-item sub-factor level, then was revised editorially to 21 factors and 96
   sub-factors — see Factor taxonomy revision.
3. **"1,651 primary / 2,746 secondary / 4,397 total."** The source file holds 1,648 primary edges —
   rids 9449, 13132 and 9809 have an empty `primary_factor` — so 4,394 total. Moot now that factor
   edges are recoded, but noted because the handoff's arithmetic was off by three regardless.

Everything else in the handoff was implemented as written.

## Open items

2. **14 pattern names in `design_patterns_papers.csv` are not in the 83-pattern catalogue**, carrying

3. **47 papers carry no v2 pattern** — 37 appear nowhere in the pattern coding, 10 lost their only

4. **85 low-confidence pattern edges and 57 low-confidence factor edges** are published, flagged

5. **67 papers previously excluded as no-fit now carry patterns** and appear in the catalogue. Their
   old exclusion reason is preserved under `legacy.no_fit_reason`.
6. **"Mindset" is broad for its one sub-factor.** F11 now holds only "acceptance and social
   perception of the agent". The name is deliberately wider than its contents; renaming later costs
   nothing, since the id and edges do not move.

## Factor taxonomy revision

An editorial pass over the factor layer, after the coding was in. Names and parents moved; the
sub-factor edges that carry the coding did not, except where listed. Full log in
`migration.factor_revisions`.

**Renamed** (id, papers and sub-factors unchanged):

| Id | Now | Was |
|---|---|---|
| F3 | Judging and deciding | Verification |
| F4 | Oversight | Transparency |
| F7 | Control and agency | Mixed-initiative interaction |
| F11 | Mindset | Social presence |
| F15 | Spatial and embodied cognition | Spatial grounding |

Each keeps `source_factor_name`. Definitions and boundary rules were rewritten against the v2
sub-factors for every factor whose label or contents moved — F3, F4, F7, F11, F13, F14, F15 — and
authored from scratch for N1–N4, which arrived in v2 with none. Every remaining boundary rule had
its sibling names refreshed, since the originals cited 1.x labels. All 21 factors now carry a
definition and a boundary rule, and no rule points at a retired or renamed sibling; both are
tested.

**F11 split.** Its three human-to-human sub-factors — social sharing and human connection, social
presence and team rapport, authenticity of AI-mediated interaction — moved to N3 Emotional state and
wellbeing, which grows to 337 papers and 6 sub-factors. F11 keeps only "acceptance and social
perception of the agent" (134 papers), hence the new name.

**F10 Rehearsal retired.** Its one sub-factor, arousal and performance, was not about rehearsal. Its
six papers each moved to a factor they already carried: 3950, 5443, 13132 to N3; 6290, 7485, 17248 to
N1. Only 5443 needed a new edge (to stress and coping, on its "led to high pressure" quote); the
other five were already coded there, so their edges were dropped as duplicates. No paper lost
coverage.

**N5 Everyday independence retired.** Its sub-factor, independence and quality of life in daily
living, reparented to F14 Accessibility, which grows to 175 papers. 28 papers kept the edge — the 26
that already carried F14, plus 3228 and 8337, both access-framed but missing an F14 edge. 24 papers
dropped it because their claim was already covered by N1, N2 or N3. One edge (14953) was dropped
outright: it rested on a background sentence about robots helping people live at home, not on
anything the paper studies.

Net: 23 factors and 97 sub-factors become **21 and 96**; 9,414 factor edges become **9,384** (1
repointed, 30 dropped, all logged individually).

## Application

The site was rebuilt as a self-contained React page; no active view assumes a scalar pattern or a
primary factor.

- Pattern pages, category pages, factor pages (with their sub-factors), paper pages, a filtered
  paper browser and a method page, all driven by the edge tables.
- Paper pages show every assigned pattern with its own evidence quote, location, role and confidence,
  the deduplicated categories reached through those patterns, and every sub-factor with its evidence.
- Filters: OR within a dimension, AND across dimensions, counts always distinct rids. Verified live —
  `pat-009` alone returns 193; with `N1` returns 117, matching the matrix cell exactly.
- The 83 × 23 matrix is grouped by category, shaded by share of each pattern's own papers, and each
  cell links to the corresponding filtered paper list.
- Legacy `#/paradigm/P1`–`P15` and `#/difficulty/F*` routes are archived: they render the current
  catalogue behind a banner naming the retired code, never silently posing as new patterns.
- The exemplar sets and the 22 cross-cutting patterns were removed as obsolete, both being anchored
  to the 15-pattern model.
